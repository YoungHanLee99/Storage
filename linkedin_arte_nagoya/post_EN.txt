I've redlined a lot of lines for this project.
This morning in Nagoya, I found one I couldn't edit. 👇

Before the doors of ARTE MUSEUM NAGOYA opened, the line was already wrapping around the building, right beneath the words "ETERNAL NATURE."

For months, this project lived on my desk as agreements, risk memos, and countless cross-border calls. Opening in a new market is never just a creative undertaking. Partnership structures, IP protection, local regulations, and trust between many stakeholders all have to come together, mostly out of sight.

Today, all of that paperwork turned into one simple scene: people waiting to step inside.

The best line I've seen in a long time. And for once, I had no redlines. ✍️

My deepest thanks to the d'strict team and our partners in Japan who made this possible.

P.S. Faces blurred. Occupational hazard of being a General Counsel. 😉
